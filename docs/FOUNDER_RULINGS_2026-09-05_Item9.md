Author: Rachel Letters, CEO. Paste to Claude Code. Register under WK-QA-018.

## The rubric scale starts at 1, and the gap was in my draft
The competence rubric runs 1 to 5. **A 1 means the domain was not demonstrated at all, whether refused, absent or not attempted.** That is a different finding from a 2, which is attempted and below standard, and a trainer needs both: one says the person did the thing badly, the other says the thing did not happen, and they call for different responses.

Add the anchor rather than renumbering the scale. Nothing already scored changes meaning, and 2 to 5 keep the words they have.

Record that the gap was in my draft rather than in the source. `rubric_anchors.csv` is my authorship, so the missing anchor is an omission of mine and not a defect in an upstream document, and a later reader should not go looking for a better copy that has it.

The machine-readable form of the anchor, so the generated form derives it rather than restating it:

anchor_1 (all domains): Not demonstrated at all: refused, absent, or not attempted.
