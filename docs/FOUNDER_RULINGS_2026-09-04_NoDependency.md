# Founder rulings, 4 September 2026: the items with no dependency

Author: Rachel Letters, CEO. Paste to Claude Code as the next instruction. Register under WK-QA-018 with the day's other entries. These are the rulings that needed nobody else; the remainder go to the 25 September two-key session.

## Ruling A. client_edit stays corporate-only, and its member half becomes a build
`client_edit` is not admitted to the member scope as a table. Half of it is the member's own words, which they should have; the other half is an internal review disposition, and a row carrying both would ship a staff judgment about the member's edit under the guise of returning their own text.

The reason field's note becomes a queue row: build a member-scope projection of the member's own submitted text, excluding the review disposition and any staff-authored field, with the projection asserted rather than hand-listed in the shape the exporter already uses. Until that row is built, `client_edit` carries its corporate-only reason as written, now naming this ruling rather than describing the question.

## Ruling B. The media manifest ships in member scope; images never do
The conflict between the portability line naming a media manifest and `legal/README.md` saying photos never appear on the client's view is resolved by the distinction the exporter already implements: a manifest of `content_sha256` and metadata is not an image. The manifest is admitted to member scope. Image bytes are never admitted to any archive at any scope, which the restore half already enforces by not writing photo rows.

`legal/README.md` gains one sentence recording the distinction, so the conflict is settled in the document that created it rather than only in the export code: photographs are never rendered to a client surface; a manifest that records that a photograph exists, with its hash and metadata and no bytes, is not a rendering and is part of the household's portable record.

Packet item B is closed and comes off the 25 September agenda.

## Ruling C. Vendor history is a queue item, not a freeze ruling
If "vendor history" in the portability line means vendor engagements rather than registry entries, nothing in the schema holds it, so there is nothing to rule about scope. Open a queue row for the missing shape and remove item C from the freeze packet, noting in the packet why it left: it was a build gap wearing the costume of a disclosure decision.

## Ruling D. The engineer retainer steps down on a trigger, not a date
When the standing authorization (Q-6 through Q-17b, with the three instruments and the loaders) completes and its exit condition is met, the senior engineer engagement moves from the build retainer to reviewer-on-call. Record the trigger in the queue's exit-condition row so it is met at the moment the condition is, rather than remembered later.

## Ruling E. Founding-member prepayment, shape only
Prepaid membership is offered as founding terms rather than as a discount scheme: six or twelve months paid at signing, at a modest discount, capped at the first ten households. The member agreement needs a mid-term refund clause and the revenue recognition treatment is Kelly's; neither is ruled here. Nothing in the software changes for this today; it is recorded so the billing shape is known before a member agreement is drafted.
