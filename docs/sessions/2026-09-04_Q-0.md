## Ground truth · disagreements, reported and not resolved

**1. THE AUTHORITY QUESTION, which contains all the others.** The package
declares itself "the build authority as of 3 September 2026" and its
CLAUDE.md is written to replace this repository's. Standing against it:
WK-DEV-006 was adopted under the two-key authorization (A567) and the
founder's own 28 August ruling says "WK-DEV-006's four phases govern";
the package cites a register namespace (A121..A133, WK-QA-015/018) that
is the LIBRARY's, not this repo's A5xx; and its consolidation sources
include documents current to early August. **The package's CLAUDE.md
cannot replace the repository's as-is even mechanically:** the
guards-manifest CI test asserts CLAUDE.md contains a row for every guard
file, and the package's copy names none of the eighteen, so the swap
fails CI structurally; beyond CI it omits operative law (the audit
invariant, the nine erasure exceptions, Ruling 1's amended capacity
exception, the verification and merging doctrine). What the package
carries that is genuinely NEW founder law (the tier ruling, quiet hours,
the Handled invariant, mode logic, fixtures, competitive inputs, brand
config, the capture-pipeline stages) deserves adoption THROUGH the
standing intake pattern: frozen, registered, and merged into the
repo's CLAUDE.md rather than over it. That is a founder call, not a
session's.

**2. The package predates a month of this repository.** Authored knowing
the early-August layer (it cites the commissioning brief and orders AQ
retrieved; both are here and frozen) and not the late-August layer:

register, G-1..G-119. Consequences by queue item:

     WK-DEV-001 on 5 August (the same staleness the 28 August status
     report corrected in the handoff); the daily sweeps' production

     envelope 0046, the drain with its A2 fix and metric, deployed).
     The package's `field_event` differs materially (before/after value
     hashes, consumed_by array, bigserial, per-household id ordering).

**3. RFC-001 versus RFC-ATTR-01: two RFCs now claim the same ground.**
This repository wrote `docs/rfc/RFC-ATTR-01_Field_Attributes.md`
(28 August, serving Gate 2 item 2 and WK-OPS-002 v1.1) and built its
step 1 on 2 September. RFC-001 §3 covers the same attributes with
DIFFERENT decisions:
   - **Knowing state**: RFC-001 says "extends the provenance enum" and
     then lists `confirmed | observed | expected | estimated | unknown`.
     The actual enum, promoted by RFC-ATTR-01 and carrying production
     data, is `asked | observed | verified_by_touch | client_written |
     unconfirmed`. These are different five-value lists sharing one
     member. One of them is wrong about what "extends" means.
   - **Materiality and consequence class**: RFC-ATTR-01 holds both as
     FOUNDER TAXONOMY, deliberately undecided; RFC-001 decides them
     (`safety_access | money_legal | convenience`; `editorial |
     behavioral | high_consequence`) and the build ruling adopts "the
     RFC-001 enum values as written". If that adoption is the founder's
     enum sign-off, it is exactly the ruling ATTR-01 was waiting for,
     and it should be recorded AGAINST ATTR-01 so one RFC survives.
     Reported, not merged.
