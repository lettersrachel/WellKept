## Ground truth

1. **The RFC-001 family namespaces do not match the live s4 kind
   namespaces**: `work.*` is a family while the shipped kinds are
   `work_item.*` and `work_requirement.*`; `decision.*` vs
   `decision_record.*`. Not reconciled silently: the catalog is a
   FORWARD namespace, the legacy kinds stay outside it and legal, and
   `catalogFamilyOf` returns null for them by design. Renaming shipped
   kinds would change shipped semantics and is nobody's to do without
   a ruling.
2. **`delight.*` is in the catalog while the Delight epic is held
   post-E4.** Dropping the name would be editing the adopted list; a
   reserved name is not a build. Both facts stated in the module.
3. **No production site writes a non-null causation_id.** Deliberate
   schema-ahead-of-writer (G-85), stated per column in the migration
   header; the first producers are the Q-12b reconciliation consumers.
4. **event_outbox.household_id itself carries no FK to household**,
   observed during the read (pre-existing, unlike situation and
   attention_record). Not changed: out of this row's scope; reported
   here rather than silently fixed or silently ignored.
5. The queue row's phrase "the family catalog as registered kinds"
   resolves to: the drain's registry stays kind-string keyed (its
   semantics sit under the A2 ruling), and a family kind registers and
   drains exactly as any kind, which is what the acceptance test
   proves. No family-wildcard registration was invented.
