# Session log: Q-12b-2, `changeset` propagation (shadow)

**Queue row:** Q-12b-2, next after Q-12b-1. **Spec:** intake
`BENCHMARK_ADOPTION.md` section 2 (verified@Q-0b, frozen) and `RFC-ATTR-01`
Amendment 1 section A1.3. **Migration:** one.

## What the spec says, quoted

Section 2: "`changeset` propagation: a source change invalidates or recomputes
dependents; safe changes apply, review-required changes route; stability
increases as execution approaches and near-term commitments lock unless a real
change occurs."

A1.3: "**Absent; new migration required.** The nearest existing mechanism (the
outbox itself) records what happened, not what a source change invalidates."

## The acceptance criterion, read carefully before building

"Given a source change (soccer moves Saturday to Sunday), when propagated,
then dependent work is recomputed, stale work is retired, safe changes apply
automatically, and exactly one genuine tradeoff reaches the member."

**Four clauses, and the fourth is the one to be careful about.** "Exactly one
genuine tradeoff reaches the member" cannot be built here: the member side is
frozen at the digest, this row is year-two `shadow`, and shadow means it
surfaces to no member at all. The honest build satisfies the first three and
records the fourth as what it is: a tradeoff is IDENTIFIED and counted, and
its delivery waits for the freeze to lift. Reported rather than quietly
reinterpreted as "one row is written somewhere".

## The plan, and the four things it deliberately does not build

**One migration, two related tables.** `changeset` (the source change and its
classification) and `changeset_effect` (one row per dependent, with the
composite `(household_id, changeset_id)` foreign key so a cross-tenant effect
is unrepresentable, the 0056 situation pattern). Two tables rather than an
array or a jsonb list, for the reason Q-12b-1 recorded one row earlier: an
array carries no foreign key and cannot hold the tenancy guarantee.

**The effect vocabulary is the spec's own two words**, `invalidated` and
`recomputed`, taken from "a source change invalidates or recomputes
dependents". No third value is added.

**The classification vocabulary is the spec's own two sets**, `safe_automatic`
and `review_required`. It ships NULLABLE WITH NO DEFAULT and the v1 producer
is a corporate operator, not a rule. **WHICH CHANGES ARE SAFE IS A SAFETY
TAXONOMY and it is the founder's**, exactly as the capture router's severity
rules and the firewall's interrupt rules are hers: five destinations shipped
and the policy produced two. An automatic classifier is stubbed by name and
not built.

**Four things this row asks for that are NOT built here, each with its
reason.**

1. **"Stale work is retired" needs a tenth `work_requirement` status and I am
   not adding one.** That CHECK carries nine states and 0051 recorded them as
   the full WK-DEV-009 section 10 lifecycle; `retired` is not among them.
   Adding a state to a shipped vocabulary is a semantics change to a shipped
   primitive, which the standing tiebreak expressly excludes from my own
   judgment. The changeset RECORDS which dependents it invalidates; acting on
   that record is the next step and it needs her word first.
2. **"Exactly one genuine tradeoff reaches the member" cannot be built**: the
   client side is frozen at the digest and this row is `shadow`, which means
   it surfaces to no member at all. A tradeoff is identified and counted; its
   delivery waits for the freeze.
3. **"Near-term commitments lock" needs a horizon, and a horizon is a
   threshold.** It ships as a null knob (`changeset_lock_horizon`, quiet while
   null, the `visit_reconciliation` and `capacity_gate` shape) rather than as a
   number I chose.
4. **"Safe changes apply automatically" has a producer only for the APPLY**,
   not for the classification that would authorize it. With classification
   operator-set, nothing applies without a person, which is the safe direction
   and the honest state.

## What was built so far, and what is still open

**IN PROGRESS.** Migration 0070 is applied locally and read back
(`changeset`, `changeset_effect`, two enums, three CHECKs, the composite
tenancy FK, four indexes), the propagation service is written, and the
full same-PR legal set is done. **The producer actions, the corporate
surface, the integration tests and the journey are not built yet**, so
this row is not finished and is not stamped as such.

**The read-before-apply rule earned its THIRD instance.** `drizzle-kit`
emitted `changeset_effect_same_household_fk` BEFORE the unique index
`changeset_household_id_key` that it references, which is exactly the
0058 and 0063 shape and exactly what Postgres refuses at apply time. The
index is moved above the FK by hand with the do-not-regenerate note in
the file's header. Three instances in three composite-FK migrations makes
this the generator's normal behaviour rather than an occasional slip.

**The four censuses were proven RED first** on the two untreated tables
(erasure, legal, archive, staff-disclosure), then green with their
treatments. `changeset_effect` is the ELEVENTH documented DELETE
exception and its deletion goes FIRST in the tool, before its parent,
because its composite foreign key points at `changeset`.

**The G-13 scope note did its job on its first working day**: adding a
staff-attributed table failed the assertion and named the new numbers
(46 tables, 19 covered, 27 not yet named), so the note could not go stale
by being forgotten.

**A second allowlist addition, reported rather than buried.** `changeset`
carries THREE staff columns and two of them are sharper than write
provenance: `classified_by` records who judged a change safe to act on
without asking, and `applied_by` records who acted. Those are judgments
attributable to a person, closer to the commitment ledger's accountable
owner than to a task-occurrence recorder, and the allowlist entry names
them as a founder line candidate for the counsel review now running.
