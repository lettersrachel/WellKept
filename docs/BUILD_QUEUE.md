The 3 September package's queue, re-cut against the Q-0 drift list per
the founder rulings on PR #282: items already built are stamped
`built@<sha>` and closed, partly built items carry their true remaining
size, new items keep the package's acceptance criteria verbatim.
Nothing is built twice. The frozen original is
`docs/intake/2026-09-03-build-package/BUILD_QUEUE.md` (superseded).

**Relationship to WORK_QUEUE.md:** WORK_QUEUE.md stays the index of the
standing state and its dated records; THIS file is the forward build
sequence. A session takes its item from here and reports into both.

**Rules (Ruling 1 and the standing Session discipline):** one item per
session, in this order, never reordered, skipped, merged or added; at
most one migration per session; `R` items are reconciliation sessions;
year-two items carry the `shadow` flag until promoted at E2 (they
compute, log and show in the corporate portal only); the launch-critical
tier ends at Q-11; year-three waits for after E4. Where Ruling 2
section 4 requires a consumer or attribute to land as its own
migration, the package row is split below with the ruling cited; those
are Part A items, not additions.

**The digest-freeze conflict is RULED (Part C §2.2, 4 September 2026):
the WK-DEV-007 freeze GOVERNS.** It was adopted under two keys and a
single-founder review does not lift it. Q-6, Q-8b and Q-9 are SPLIT,
not deferred: their freeze-safe halves proceed in their queue
positions, and any member-facing surface carries the `FREEZE-GATED`
label and waits for the two-key decision scheduled with the COO for
the 25 September session, whose scope is exactly whether the freeze
lifts for these three surfaces at E1 and nothing else. Until then the
queue assumes neither outcome. The split is written into each row
below.
