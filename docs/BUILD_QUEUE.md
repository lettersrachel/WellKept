## Exit condition, and what it triggers

**This section did not exist when ruling D asked for it.** Ruling D
(4 September 2026, `docs/FOUNDER_RULINGS_2026-09-04_NoDependency.md`)
says to record the retainer trigger "in the queue's exit-condition row",
and the queue had no such row: the exit condition was carried in the
standing authorization and in the founder's head rather than written
where it would be noticed at the moment it is met. Reported rather than
quietly invented, and then created, because the whole point of the
ruling is that the condition should be met at the moment it is true
rather than remembered later.

**The condition.** The standing authorization of 4 September 2026 runs
Q-6 through Q-17b, with the three instruments and the loaders. It
completes when every row in that span is built or explicitly closed and
its own exit condition is met.

**What happens then, by ruling D:** the senior engineer engagement moves
from the BUILD RETAINER to REVIEWER ON CALL. The move is triggered by
the condition, not by a date.

**Who checks.** Whoever closes the last row in the span says so in that
session's log and in this section, in the same change. It is a sentence,
and the reason it is written here is that a trigger nobody is told to
look for is a date in disguise.

**The venue for the condition's named test is a FIXTURE HOUSEHOLD, never
a live one** (founder ruling Part One item 5, 4 September 2026). The
reason is stated so it is not softened later: the exit condition must not
depend on founder content landing first. A fixture is the venue, and the
test's OUTPUT is itself informative, because what it cannot produce tells
the founder which content is still missing for it to produce real
results. That also keeps the condition inside the standing fixtures rule:
no real household data, Fernbrook DEMO and the Smoke Test Fixture, and
the Trainor training household.
